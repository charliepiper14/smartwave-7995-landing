Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is $7,995 really the total price?

Yes. $7,995 is the fixed price for our 3.1 kW premium system, including installation, permits and the 30-year manufacturer warranty. No hidden fees.

What if I want a bigger system or a battery?

You can add more panels or a battery, which costs more than $7,995. Additional discounts are available for larger systems, and we'll give you an exact price before you commit. There is no obligation to add anything.

How many panels are included?

Between 7 and 10 premium panels, depending on the wattage of the panels used.

How much could I save?

Our design software calculates the figure for your home. For the average home, the estimated electric bill savings over 30 years is $35,000.

What does the 30-year warranty cover?

The manufacturer guarantees your solar panels' power output for 30 years.

What if my panels underproduce?

We guarantee the output. If your panels produce less electricity than our guarantee, we add more for free.

Is financing available?

Yes. Qualified homeowners can go solar with $0 down through our financing options.
